# Module 1 — Independent Workplace Communication Assignments

**Instructions:** Complete individually unless faculty assigns a team. Use fictional data only. Submit one PDF or Markdown file per task. File name `A01_student-id.pdf` etc. Include scenario, first draft, final response, reasoning (100–150 words), and reflection (50–100 words). Do not submit real employee/customer data. Cite external sources and disclose AI use.

**Common assessment (100):** clarity 20; language 15; tone 15; completeness 20; situational judgement 20; structure 10. For speaking tasks faculty additionally assess delivery/listening using a published adaptation.

## A01 — Fix the Email
**Scenario:** HR payroll reminder.

**Your task:** Rewrite an accusatory reminder into a respectful message with subject, action, deadline and support contact.

**Submit:** (1) a response addressed to the correct audience, (2) 100–150 words explaining channel/tone/risks, (3) first draft and revised draft with two improvements highlighted.

**Check before submission:** Are facts verified? Is the audience clear? Is there a concrete next action? Are promises realistic? Is the language respectful?

## Worked illustration: A01
**Poor message:** 'You people are not sending documents properly. Do the needful ASAP.'

**Improved illustrative message:**

**Subject:** Action required: pending payroll documents by 24 November

Dear Team, Some payroll documents remain outstanding. Please submit the required documents by 5 PM on 24 November so that processing can proceed on schedule. If you have already submitted them, please disregard this reminder. If you need help, contact the HR team. Regards, Ramya, HR Executive.

**Why improved:** Identifies action and deadline, avoids blame, offers help. The date is fictional and must be adapted to the case. Do not copy the model as your own response.
