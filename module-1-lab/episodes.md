# Malnad Connect Pvt. Ltd. — Eight Connected Episodes

**Fictional company:** Karnataka retail distribution and customer support. Characters: Ramya (HR), Hareesh (Sales), Ramesh (Operations), General Manager and customer representatives. These are instructional simulations, not actual corporate incidents.
