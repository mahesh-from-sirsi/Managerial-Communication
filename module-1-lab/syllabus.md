# Module 1 Syllabus — Foundations of Managerial Communication

**Course:** VTU MBA Managerial Communication (3MBA106)  
**Module:** 1 | **Teaching hours:** 8

## Prescribed syllabus

> Module 1: Foundations of Managerial Communication Explain the meaning, definition, role, classification, and purpose of communication. Communication process and characteristics of effective communication. Importance of communication in management and organizational structures. Role of communication in conflict resolution, crisis management, and negotiation. Communication practices in cross-cultural settings. Barriers to effective communication and remedies.
