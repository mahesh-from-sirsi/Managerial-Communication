# Module 5 Syllabus — Case Analysis, Etiquette & Legal Aspects

**Course:** VTU MBA Managerial Communication (3MBA106)  
**Module:** 5 | **Teaching hours:** 8

## Prescribed syllabus

> Module 5: Case Analysis, Etiquette & Legal Aspects. Interpret and apply the case method for managerial learning and decision-making. Analyze business cases using structured approaches and frameworks. Best practices and challenges in case preparation and presentation. Role and advantages of etiquette in managerial communication. Legal aspects such as invasion of privacy, misrepresentation, and fraud in communication.

*Based on the syllabus provided by the faculty. Line breaks and punctuation normalised.*

- [ ] Case method for managerial learning

- [ ] Case preparation best practices

- [ ] Role of etiquette in managerial communication
