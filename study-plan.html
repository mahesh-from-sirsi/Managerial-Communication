How to study each module

  1. Read the module scope and identify key terms.
  2. Study one concept at a time; explain it in your own words.
  3. Connect each concept to a college, local business or workplace situation.
  4. Attempt the case study and prepare a short recommendation.
  5. Practise 3-mark definitions, 7-mark explanations and 10-mark applications.
  6. Complete the MCQ quiz and revisit incorrect answers.

Inclusive learning approach

Use simple English, sentence starters and pair practice before whole-class speaking. Students can first organise thoughts in a comfortable language and then practise expressing them in clear professional English. Accent is not a measure of intelligence. Focus on clarity, listening, evidence and improvement.
