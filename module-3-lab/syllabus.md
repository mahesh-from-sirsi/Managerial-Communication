# Module 3 Syllabus — Written & Employment Communication

**Course:** VTU MBA Managerial Communication (3MBA106)  
**Module:** 3 | **Teaching hours:** 8

## Prescribed syllabus

> Module 3: Written & Employment Communication apply principles of clarity and effectiveness in business writing. Utilize the 3X3 writing process (pre-writing, writing, revising) in business communication. Analyzing audience needs to craft appropriate positive, neutral, persuasive, and negative messages. Creating various business documents including letters, employee reviews, and recommendation letters. Developing professional CVs and demonstrate group discussion and interview skills. Impact of technology-enabled communication tools (email, intranet, video conferencing, etc.).

*Based on the syllabus provided by the faculty. Line breaks and punctuation normalised.*

## Topic-wise learning checklist

- [ ] Clarity and effectiveness in business writing
- [ ] 3×3 writing process: pre-writing
- [ ] 3×3 writing process: writing
- [ ] 3×3 writing process: revising
- [ ] Audience analysis and message adaptation
- [ ] Positive and neutral messages
- [ ] Persuasive and negative messages
- [ ] Business letters and documents
- [ ] Employee reviews and recommendation letters
- [ ] Professional CV preparation
- [ ] Group discussion skills
- [ ] Interview skills
- [ ] Technology-enabled communication: email, intranet, video conferencing and other tools
